About our hospital

A legacy of trust, with care at the centre.

Bindhya Multispeciality Hospital serves the Rajapalayam community with a focus on respectful, compassionate care. Our team and facilities support a range of medical and diagnostic needs.

  • Patient-focused care and guidance
  • Multiple medical specialities
  • Diagnostic and treatment facilities
  • Emergency services available 24 hours a day
